What comes from KerBalloons
---------------------------

RealBalloons is a heavily rewritten derivative of KerBalloons (originally by JoePatrick1, maintained
by linuxgurugamer) and KerBalloons Reinflated. It includes:

  - GameData/RealBalloons/Parts/RealBalloon/balloon125.mu   the KerBalloons balloon model, unchanged
  - GameData/RealBalloons/Parts/RealBalloon/KBTexKerbin.dds the KerBalloons balloon texture, modified
                                                            (planet picture on the cap painted out,
                                                            teal glow ring made metal grey)
  - GameData/RealBalloons/Parts/RealBalloon/RealBalloons_Envelope.dds
                                                            the KerBalloons balloon fabric, unrolled
                                                            for the plugin's envelope mapping
  - GameData/RealBalloons/Parts/RealBalloon/RealBalloons_Cap.dds
                                                            the KerBalloons cap art, enlarged to fill
                                                            its own texture
  - GameData/RealBalloons/Sounds/*.wav                      the KerBalloons inflate, deflate (now the rip
                                                            panel's) and burst sounds
  - Source/                                                 the plugin, which began as KerBalloons
                                                            Reinflated's ModuleKerBalloon and keeps its
                                                            inflation framework
